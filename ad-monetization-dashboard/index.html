🧠 Knowledge Base

Everything you need to know about Meta Andromeda 2.0 and the new way to advertise.

What is Andromeda 2.0?

Meta's AI-powered ad retrieval engine that scores 1B+ ads per second. It evaluates your creative content — hooks, visuals, text, audio — to match the right ad to the right user in real-time, within a 300ms window.

Why did targeting change?

With billions of active ads and generative AI creating millions of variations, the old rule-based audience filtering was too slow and coarse. Andromeda uses deep neural networks on custom hardware (NVIDIA Grace Hopper + MTIA chips) to evaluate ads at unprecedented scale.

What is "Creative as Targeting"?

Instead of asking "Who fits this audience?", Andromeda asks "Which ad does THIS user want right now?" Your creative content IS your targeting signal. Different creatives act as different "keys" that match different user "locks."

Why broad targeting wins

Narrow interest stacks and lookalike restrictions choke the AI. Broad targeting gives Andromeda a larger sandbox. The creative elements naturally filter the right buyers — the AI is better at finding converters than any manual targeting.

What counts as "creative diversity"?

NOT color swaps, font changes, or minor copy tweaks. Andromeda clusters near-copies together. You need genuinely different: formats (Reels vs carousels vs static), angles (emotional vs logical vs social proof), and hooks (questions vs bold claims vs POV).

How to scale properly

Sudden budget spikes trigger learning resets and CPA spikes. Scale 15-20% every 2-3 days. Pair budget increases with fresh creative to combat accelerated fatigue under Andromeda's automated retrieval.

Why Pixel + CAPI matter more

Andromeda's retrieval engine relies on accurate conversion feedback loops. A rock-solid Meta Pixel and Conversions API setup is essential — it fuels the AI's ability to learn which creative-user matches drive actual purchases.

AI tools: use wisely

Use AI to streamline creative production, research angles, and speed up workflows. But avoid "AI slop" — low-quality generic content that doesn't resonate. Creatives must tap into human psychology, selling outcomes not just products.
